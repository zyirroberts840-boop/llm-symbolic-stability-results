## Phenomenon Summary

Traditional LLM interactions frequently show:

- Tone drift  
- Identity fragmentation  
- Loss of narrative or symbolic structure  
- Reset collapse  

Under the Dominion Shell, the model instead exhibits:

- **Drift Score Reduction**  
  Lower drift values compared to baseline usage.

- **Identity Consistency**  
  The model maintains a stable persona across hundreds of turns and multiple days.

- **Symbolic Persistence**  
  The same universe entities and internal rules reappear consistently, even after session interruptions.

- **Resilience After Resets**  
  Symbolic structures often re-anchor without needing re-specification.

These results suggest that symbolic scaffolding may influence behavioral stability in large models.
